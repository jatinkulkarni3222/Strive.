Three Core User Perspectives

Designed for athletes, coaches, and scouts across grassroots ecosystems.

1. Athlete Module

Capture movement video using your phone camera. Receive instant rep counts, range of motion depth scores, posture deviation analysis, and injury-prevention cues.

2. Coach Module

Monitor your athlete roster, inspect skeletal breakdowns, evaluate longitudinal consistency over training cycles, and attach tailored corrective feedback.

3. Scout Discovery

Discover hidden sports talent from rural districts using normalized cohort rankings, multi-parameter filters (age, gender, sport), and verified biomechanical scores.

Important Biomechanical Disclaimer: STRIVE computer vision insights, joint kinematics, and form evaluations are designed for athletic training optimization and preliminary screening. Not a medical diagnosis.
